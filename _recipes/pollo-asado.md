- Toast the cumin and coriander and place in the blender.
- Add all remaining ingredients, except chicken, to the blender. Blend until pureed. 
- Place chicken in mixing bowl; add all of the blended marinade and mix until well coated. Marinate for 30 to 40 minutes--no longer, or the chicken will become mealy. 
- Prep the charcoal grill for direct heat (no deflectors) with all charcoal or with a small amount of wood; allow to come to temperature while the chicken marinates. 
- Grill the chicken:
	- Oil or spray the grates before placing the chicken down. 
	- Lay the thighs out flat in a single layer.
	- Flip after there are good, black char marks. 
- Prep for serving:
	- Let the chicken rest at least 15 minutes. 
	- Slice the thighs into thin strips against the grain.
	- Serve with tortillas.
